Background

I direct, shoot, cut, grade and finish. Mostly I listen.

I’ve filmed the International Criminal Court’s visit to Sudan and campaign films for a blockchain. The tools are mine; the stories belong to the people in them. English and Arabic. Based in Dubai.

My photographs have run in The Telegraph and on the International Criminal Court’s own channels.

القصة أمانة — a story is a trust.
