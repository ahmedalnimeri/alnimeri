Privacy

What this site records.

Plainly, and only what is true of this site. Last updated 30 July 2026.

What is recorded

When you open a page here, the server writes one row containing:

  • your IP address
  • the approximate city and country your IP resolves to, and your network operator
  • the page you opened and the site you arrived from, if any
  • your browser and operating system, as reported by your browser
  • the date and time

Requests for images, fonts and stylesheets are not recorded — only pages.

Why

This is a personal portfolio. The log exists so I can see whether people are finding it and where they came from. It is not used for advertising, it is not combined with anything else, and it is never sold or shared.

How long it is kept

Ninety days. Older rows are deleted automatically — this is enforced in the code that writes the log, not merely stated here.

Cookies

This site sets none. Visitor statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is aggregate and cookie-free — which is why you were not asked to accept anything.

Video

Films play through Vimeo, embedded with Do Not Track enabled, so Vimeo does not build a profile of you from this site. Vimeo still receives the request needed to deliver the video, under its own privacy policy. Following a link out to X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or LinkedIn takes you into their terms, not mine.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes requests on my behalf and keeps its own operational logs under its privacy policy.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, EU, or another region with equivalent law, you can ask what is held about you and ask for it to be deleted. Email ahmed@alnimeri.com from the network you visited on, or tell me the approximate date and time, and I will find and remove it. Given the ninety-day window, it may already be gone.
