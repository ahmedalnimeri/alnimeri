Privacy

What this site records.

Last updated 4 October 2026.

What is recorded

When you open a page here, the server writes one row containing:

  • your IP address
  • the approximate city and country your IP resolves to, and your network operator
  • the page you opened and the site you arrived from, if any
  • your browser and operating system, as reported by your browser
  • the date and time

Requests for images, fonts and stylesheets are not recorded — only pages.

The site also notes a few things done on it — pressing Play on a film, opening or sending the contact form, following a view count to its post, sharing a reel link, downloading the CV — as one line each holding what happened, the film, the page, how it was done or where a shared link came from, and your country, with no IP address and no browser details, deleted after ninety days.

Why

This is a personal portfolio. The log exists so I can see whether people are finding it and where they came from. It is not used for advertising, it is not combined with anything else, and it is never sold or shared.

How long it is kept

Ninety days. Older rows are deleted automatically — this is enforced in the code that writes the log, not merely stated here.

Cookies

None. Visitor statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is aggregate and sets no cookies, so you were not asked to accept anything.

Local time

The line above the headline naming your city and your clock, and the time beside the contact line, are written at the edge from the same geolocation Cloudflare attaches to every request; it is read once to render the page and is not kept beyond the visit log described above. Crawlers get the page without any of it.

Reel links

You can select films and make a link to your own reel, such as alnimeri.com/reel/5a3. The link is the reel: one character per film, in your order. Nothing else is encoded in it and nothing is stored on the server when you make one (copying or sharing it is noted as above) — your selection lives in your browser's own storage until you clear it. Opening a reel link is logged like any other page view, described above.

Video

Films play through Vimeo, embedded with Do Not Track enabled, so Vimeo does not build a profile of you from this site. Two music videos, Al Eid Shofa and Ma Tsheely Hamm, play instead through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com), which loads only when you press Play. Each still receives the request needed to deliver the video, under its own privacy policy: Vimeo’s and Google’s. Following a link out to X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube or IMDb takes you into their terms, not mine.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes requests on my behalf and keeps its own operational logs under its privacy policy.

The contact form

The form behind “Get in touch” saves what you type — your name, company, what the film is about, who it is for, its timing, the film or the selected films you came from and where a shared link to the form came from, the email or WhatsApp number you give, and the message the form writes from them — in this site’s own database on Cloudflare, so it reaches me. With it go your IP address (so no more than five briefs an hour come from one sender), your country and your browser. It is kept for a year and used only to reply to you. If saving fails, you’re offered a link to send the same message from your own email app; nothing is sent until you send it.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, EU, or another region with equivalent law, you can ask what is held about you and ask for it to be deleted. Email ahmed@alnimeri.com from the network you visited on, or tell me the approximate date and time, and I will find and remove it. Visit rows are kept for ninety days and briefs for a year, so it may already be gone.
