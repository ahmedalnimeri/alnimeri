About

003

Every brief has a story buried in it. My job is finding it.

Eleven years across commercial, documentary and institutional work — from the ICC Prosecutor’s visit to Sudan to a sit-in in Khartoum to a stage in Abu Dhabi.

Since 2023 I have made films for Solana at 1000media, part of Nas Company — sixty-six published pieces, 22.4 million verified views, twelve of them past half a million and two past a million. Before that, campaign and documentary work across Sudan and the Gulf.

I work in English and Arabic, and I travel.
