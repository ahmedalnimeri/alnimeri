Behind the work

Eleven years of commercial, documentary and institutional work, including the ICC Prosecutor’s visit to Sudan, a sit-in in Khartoum and a stage in Abu Dhabi.

Since 2023 I have made films for Solana at 1000media, part of Nas Company: sixty-six published films, 22.4 million views between them, eleven past half a million and two past a million. Before that, campaign and documentary work across Sudan and the Gulf.

I work in English and Arabic.
