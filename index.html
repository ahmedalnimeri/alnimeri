Behind the work

Every brief has a story buried in it. My job is finding it.

Eleven years across commercial, documentary and institutional work — from the ICC Prosecutor’s visit to Sudan to a sit-in in Khartoum to a stage in Abu Dhabi.

Since 2023 I have made films for Solana at 1000media, part of Nas Company — sixty-six published pieces, 22.4 million verified views, eleven of them past half a million and two past a million. Before that, campaign and documentary work across Sudan and the Gulf.

I work in English and Arabic, and I travel.

BEFORE THE BRIEF

Working with a film director in Dubai

What kinds of films do you make?

I work across brand and campaign films, event and conference films, documentaries, institutional films and post-production. Explore Solana Accelerate for campaign work, Al Doroub for documentary, or the full film selection.

Do you work in English and Arabic?

Yes. I work in English and Arabic, from discussing a brief through direction and editorial. I’m based in Dubai, with experience across Sudan and the Gulf.

Can I book direction or editing separately?

Yes. A brief can call for direction, cinematography, editing or a combination. My work also includes creative direction, motion graphics, colour and sound. See my experience and credits.

How much does a project cost?

Fees are quoted per project, based on the scope, production requirements and deliverables. Send the audience, format, location, timing and budget range if known so we can discuss the right approach.

How do I start a film project?

Send the brief with what you want to make, who it is for, where it will run and your target delivery date. A first idea is enough to start the conversation.
