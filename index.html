About

I make films that hold attention without shouting for it.

I direct, shoot, cut, grade and finish. Eleven years across commercial, documentary and institutional work — from a courtroom in The Hague to a sit-in in Khartoum to a stage in Abu Dhabi.

Since 2024 I have led creative and social for Solana at 1000media, part of Nas Company. Sixty-plus films, north of twenty million views, several past a million on their own. Before that, campaign and documentary work across Sudan and the Gulf.

I work in English and Arabic, and I travel.
