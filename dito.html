Dito Daily

One word a day, in nine languages.

A small automated studio I built and run from Dubai.

What it is

Dito Daily is a set of fourteen social channels that make and post their own short videos every day on Facebook and Instagram. Nine teach a language, one word or phrase at a time: French, German, Spanish, Russian, Korean, Japanese, Arabic, Italian and Turkish. The others carry Qur’an recitation and du’aa, football and news.

How it works

Each channel runs on its own schedule on a single server. It picks a word from a reviewed corpus, renders a card with pronunciation, an example sentence and a voiced reading, and posts it as a reel, a story and, a few days a week, a quiz. Words come back for review after three weeks, and each week follows a theme.

Nothing is posted unchecked. Every word, definition, example and pronunciation is read before it goes into a corpus, and entries that fail are kept out.

Where Claude fits

The language work — checking new vocabulary, catching wrong-sense definitions, writing captions and quiz options, and answering comments — is the part that needs a model that is accurate outside English. That is the work Dito Daily is moving to Claude.
